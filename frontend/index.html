Clinical Focus

Why MediMate AI for Post-Accident Recovery?

MediMate AI addresses the massive outpatient gap in post-accident trauma recovery. Over 85% of accident survivors experience persistent whiplash, joint stiffness, and muscle spasms for 6 to 12 weeks after hospital discharge.

At home, patients struggle with uncertainty: "When do I apply ice vs heat?", "Are these exercises safe?", and "Is this pain normal?". MediMate AI provides continuous quantitative tracking with a daily VAS 1-10 pain slider, 105 clinical Q&A AI assistance, and real-time tele-rehab video sessions with supervising physiotherapists.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MediMate AI Post-Accident Pain & Rehabilitation System.

What are the 3 dedicated user logins in this system? +
The platform has exactly 3 dedicated roles: (1) Patient Portal for daily VAS 1-10 pain score logging, ROM mobility tracking, and guided home exercises; (2) Physiotherapist Portal for reviewing patient recovery velocity curves, ML chronic pain risk alerts, and custom exercise prescriptions; and (3) Rehab Clinic Admin for specialist allocation, TENS/rehab equipment inventory, and clinic recovery rate analytics.
How does Preethi AI's 105 Clinical Q&A Engine assist recovering patients? +
Preethi AI is loaded with 105 peer-reviewed physiotherapy protocols covering post-accident recovery (whiplash, fractures, sprains), ice vs heat R.I.C.E. guidelines, safe home exercises, VAS 1-10 pain scale interpretation, and critical red-flag emergency detection (DVT, compartment syndrome) with instant 108 SOS dispatch.
How does the Tele-Rehab Google Meet Video Suite work? +
Patients and physiotherapists can join a virtual video consultation directly in the browser via WebRTC. It provides camera toggle, microphone control, screen sharing, in-call clinical chat, live call timers, and a prescribed exercise checklist overlay for posture alignment checks.
How does Machine Learning predict recovery trajectory and chronic pain risk? +
Our supervised Random Forest Classifier evaluates patient age, days since accident, daily pain score (VAS), exercise adherence %, Range of Motion (ROM %), and swelling grade to classify recovery into On-Track (Low Risk), Delayed (Moderate Risk), or High Chronic Pain Risk with 99.17% validation accuracy.
Can I test all 3 logins easily during demonstration? +
Yes! Our Unified Login page (login.html) features 1-click demo fast-fill chips for Patient (patient@medimate.ai), Physiotherapist (physio@medimate.ai), and Admin (admin@medimate.ai) for instantaneous evaluation.
