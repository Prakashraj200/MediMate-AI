The Mission

What is MediMate AI?

MediMate AI is an all-in-one clinical operating system engineered to eliminate the fragmentation that plagues modern hospitals. In traditional healthcare systems, patient records, diagnostic laboratories, physician appointments, and pharmacy dispensation function in isolated silos.

MediMate AI creates a continuous, secure data bridge between every stakeholder. A patient books an appointment online; Preethi AI provides preliminary triage; the physician reviews the chart and issues an electronic prescription; the laboratory automatically receives diagnostic requisitions; and the pharmacy dispenses verified medications with real-time batch expiration safety checks.

⚡ Dual-Engine Auth

Works effortlessly with Firebase Cloud Auth or local browser fallback for uninterrupted clinical uptime.

🔒 HIPAA Standard

Designed around strict patient privacy, data integrity, and role-segregated medical records access.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the MediMate AI clinical operating platform.

What is Preethi AI and how does it assist clinicians? +
Preethi AI is our specialized healthcare neural assistant. It parses patient symptom narratives in real time, computes clinical risk scores (Low, Moderate, Urgent), highlights potential drug interactions, and suggests appropriate hospital departments. It acts as a safety copilot to assist licensed doctors rather than replacing clinical judgment.
Can I test the platform without an internet connection? +
Yes! MediMate AI includes an automated dual-engine authentication and storage system. If Firebase Cloud services are unreachable or running locally, the system seamlessly transitions to synchronized local clinical registries so workflows never halt.
How does the Smart Pharmacy Expiry Radar work? +
The Smart Pharmacy module constantly tracks batch manufacturing dates and shelf-life horizons. Any medication within 30 days of expiration is automatically flagged with high-visibility warnings, preventing inadvertent dispensing and optimizing hospital reordering cycles.
How do patients receive their diagnostic lab reports? +
As soon as a certified laboratory pathologist records and signs off on test results in the Lab Portal, the patient's Electronic Health Record is updated instantly with real-time notifications and downloadable report documents.
How is patient medical privacy protected? +
Every data transfer uses military-grade AES-256 encryption. Our strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) architecture guarantees that laboratory technicians only see diagnostic requests, pharmacists only view prescription fulfillment records, and doctors only access authorized patient health files.
