Step 3: Prescribed Exercises (இன்றைய 4 உடற்பயிற்சிகள்)

Today's Recovery Routine

0 of 4 Done

Prescribed by Dr. Anand Kumar for Day 14 Recovery. Check off each exercise after completing it.

1
Neck Chin Tucks (கழுத்து பயிற்சி) 3 Sets x 10 Reps • Relieves neck whiplash & stiffness

Sit up straight. Pull your chin gently backwards (making a subtle double chin). Hold for 5 seconds, then relax.

2
Knee Press / Quad Sets (முழங்கால் பயிற்சி) 3 Sets x 12 Reps • Strengthens leg without joint friction

Place a small rolled towel under your knee. Press the back of your knee firmly down into the towel. Hold for 5s.

3
Pelvic Bridge (இடுப்பு மற்றும் முதுகு) 2 Sets x 10 Reps • Relieves lower spine pressure

Lie on your back with knees bent. Tighten stomach muscles and gently lift hips slightly upward. Hold 5s.

4
Arm Pendulum Circles (தோள்பட்டை பயிற்சி) 3 Sets x 15 Circles • Relaxes joint with gentle gravity

Lean forward slightly, support yourself on a table. Let your arm dangle freely like a pendulum and sway in gentle circles.
