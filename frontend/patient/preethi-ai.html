Hello Prakashraj. Based on your Day 4 post-collision stage and VAS pain rating of 7/10, you are currently in the acute inflammatory phase of cervical sprain/whiplash recovery.

1. Ice vs Heat Recommendation:

  • Apply Ice (Cryotherapy): Apply a cold compress wrapped in a damp towel for 15–20 minutes every 3 to 4 hours. Cold causes vasoconstriction, effectively suppressing localized swelling and inflammatory mediators around C5–C6.
  • Avoid Heat Pads: Heat increases microvascular permeability and vasogenic edema during the first 5–7 days following acute impact. Save thermotherapy for subacute muscular stiffness.

2. Immediate Safe Gentle Protocols:

  • Gentle Isometric Chin Tucks: In an upright seated posture, gently slide your chin straight backward without nodding downward. Hold for 3 seconds. Repeat 5 times within pain-free tolerance.
  • Scapular Retraction: Draw both shoulder blades gently backward and down for 5 seconds to reduce levator scapulae and trapezius spasm.
  • Ergonomic Rest: Support the cervical lordotic curve with a rolled towel or orthopedic contour pillow.
⚠️ Emergency Red-Flag Warning: If you experience progressive numbness radiating down your arm into your fingers, sudden hand weakness, dizziness, or dysphagia, discontinue exercises and seek emergency medical evaluation immediately.
